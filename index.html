    Voice rules+
    01Hinglish in Roman script; English tech nouns kept as-is. Always tum / tumhe / tumhara, never "aap".
    02Short clauses joined by commas, almost no full stops. One action per clause.
    03House spellings: krna krlo krdo krlena rkhlo skte ho bas hogya baki toh fir yeh yahan niche.
    04Steps are taps and UI labels, never explanations of why.
    05Never open with a greeting, "aaj main", or "hello dosto".
    Triple-Stack CTA+
    1Comment keyword → DM: comment "keyword" aur main tumhe DM krdunga (or pin the name in comments).
    2Save with a reason: isse save krlo taaki baad mein kaam aaye.
    3Share to a specific person: apne editor dost ko bhej do.
    4Then baki follow bhi krlena, then the sign-off byeeeeeeee....... — never shortened.
